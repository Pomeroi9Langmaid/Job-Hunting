## Draft SIMPLE BONUS STRUCTURE — discussion only
This is a draft framework, not a claimed market norm or proposed final percentage.

1. **Eligible business:** new customers/new business directly sourced, led or materially closed by the employee, recorded and agreed in writing; define treatment of partner-led and existing-account expansion.
2. **One deal value definition:** use **first 12 months' contracted gross profit** for project/equipment or mixed projects, or first 12 months' contracted recurring revenue for a pure service subscription. Choose one basis suitable to DANFO's margins **before** setting a rate. Avoid confusing revenue with profit.
3. **Simple commission formula:** commission = agreed commission rate × agreed first-year value basis × term multiplier. Commission rate to be negotiated after role, economics and typical deal size are understood.
4. **Committed-term recognition:** illustrative multipliers: 1 year = 1.00×; 2 years = 1.25×; 3 years = 1.50×; 5+ years = 2.00×. This rewards binding value without paying on cancellable or hypothetical years. Apply only to non-cancellable minimum commitments; pro-rate for unusual terms if needed.
5. **Earned and paid:** commission earned upon binding signature and completion of explicit qualification checks; **50% paid in next monthly payroll, 50% after first customer payment**, normally no later than the following payroll. Alternative: entire commission next payroll after first payment if company insists. No routine financial-year wait.
6. **Cancellations / non-payment:** narrow, time-limited reversal provisions for genuine early cancellation/default; no arbitrary discretionary reduction after earning.
7. **Exit protection:** earned commission survives departure; a documented tail period (e.g. 3–6 months) covers deals principally sourced and negotiated before exit which sign afterward.
8. **Annual bonus:** if desired, add a modest separate strategic/target bonus, never replacing earned individual deal commissions.
9. **Written policy:** clear examples, crediting rules, definitions, payroll dates, cap/no cap, VAT treatment and currency; avoid EBITDA/MRR/churn/operations weighted scorecards unrelated to individual control.

**Worked illustration only:** with a SEK 500,000 first-year commissionable value and a hypothetical 5% commission rate, 1-year payout SEK 25,000, 3-year payout SEK 37,500 (1.5×), 5-year payout SEK 50,000 (2×). These numbers are not agreed and should be adjusted for actual commercial margins.
