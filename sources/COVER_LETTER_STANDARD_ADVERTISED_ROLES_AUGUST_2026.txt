ANDREW LANGMAID - ADVERTISED ROLE COVER LETTER STANDARD
Version 2 - 2 September 2026

PURPOSE

This Source governs the drafting and formatting of cover letters for advertised job applications.

MANDATORY TEMPLATE LOCK

Every advertised-role cover letter MUST use Andrew's established MASTER cover-letter template/layout as the immutable visual starting point.

- Never redesign, rebuild from scratch, substitute a generic template, or reinterpret the visual structure.
- Preserve the established typography, spacing, margins, header/contact treatment, one-page structure and overall visual hierarchy unless Andrew explicitly requests a redesign in the current conversation.
- Tailoring means changing the role-specific wording inside the existing template, not changing the template itself.
- Do not print a raw URL to Andrew's personal Family Archive/AI project in the cover letter unless Andrew explicitly asks for the URL to appear. It may be described as supporting evidence without the direct link.
- Preserve Andrew's primary positioning as a hands-on full-cycle international sales and new-business professional. AI/personal-project evidence is supportive, never the main career identity.
- OUTPUT NAMING: final PDF filenames must NEVER include MASTER or TEMPLATE. Default cover filename: Andrew_Langmaid_Cover_[Company].pdf. Add a short role suffix only when needed to distinguish multiple applications to the same company.
- Before delivery, visually render and inspect the completed one-page cover against the established template.

MANDATORY OPENING EXPLANATION

Every advertised-role cover letter must explain Andrew's reason for applying using this exact sentence unless Andrew explicitly changes it:

"My role with Smartvel is coming to an end, and I am deliberately looking for a permanent, hands-on international sales and new-business position."

Do not replace this with generic wording such as "organisational changes", "post-acquisition restructuring", "seeking a new challenge" or similar. Do not invent a different reason for leaving.

STANDARD POSITIONING STRUCTURE

The cover should make Andrew's commercial story immediately clear, using the same core positioning proven in speculative outreach:

1. Entrepreneurial drive
- Founded and self-financed Pomeroi Fine Jewellers in the UK.
- Established Luxury Sweden, wrote the business plan, secured Swedish government start-up funding and agreed distribution terms with several brands.

2. Commercial results
- Increased UK turnover at Padani by approximately 80% in the first year.
- At ArrivalGuides, personalised B2B outreach generated 320% more qualified opportunities and scheduled meetings in the first six months of 2020 than in the comparable 2019 period.
- Achieved the annual commercial target for a newly introduced platform within six months.

3. Earned trust
- After eight years helping to develop an established central London business, its owner asked Andrew to take it over.
- After delivering growth at Padani, Andrew was invited to become a partner.
- In 2022, ArrivalGuides' owners asked Andrew to stabilise the company and lead its sale.

4. Continued hands-on selling
- After ArrivalGuides was acquired by Smartvel, approximately 80% of Andrew's work remained hands-on international new business.
- Andrew still personally handles target-account selection, outreach, discovery, tailored demonstrations, proposals, negotiation, contracts and close.

ROLE-SPECIFIC TAILORING

The opening paragraph should first state why the specific role is relevant, then include the mandatory Smartvel sentence verbatim.

After the standard positioning evidence, add a concise role-specific section explaining the strongest direct match to the vacancy. Use only evidence Andrew can truthfully support. Do not manufacture sector experience, buyer networks, technical credentials or language proficiency.

Where there is a bridgeable gap, acknowledge it plainly and explain the closest transferable evidence. Do not hide the gap and do not over-emphasise it.

STYLE

- One page.
- Clean Arial typography throughout.
- Direct, senior and commercially credible.
- Avoid generic cover-letter language and empty enthusiasm.
- Make the reason for applying and the four-part commercial story obvious without requiring the reader to infer them.
- Preserve Andrew's primary identity as a hands-on full-cycle international sales and new-business professional.

FINAL RULE

Future advertised-role covers should use this Source and Andrew's established MASTER cover template as the default standard unless Andrew explicitly instructs otherwise in the current conversation.
