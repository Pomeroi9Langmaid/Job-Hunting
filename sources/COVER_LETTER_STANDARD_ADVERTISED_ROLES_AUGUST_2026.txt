ANDREW LANGMAID - ADVERTISED ROLE COVER LETTER STANDARD
Version 4 - 28 October 2026

PURPOSE

This Source governs the drafting and formatting of cover letters for advertised job applications.

MANDATORY TEMPLATE LOCK

Every advertised-role cover letter MUST use the CV-matched visual system expressly approved on 23 October 2026 (superseding the earlier unmodified cover exemplar).

- Do not substitute a generic template or ignore the approved 23 October CV-matched visual system.
- Preserve the established typography, spacing, margins, header/contact treatment, one-page structure and overall visual hierarchy unless Andrew explicitly requests a redesign in the current conversation.
- Tailoring means changing the role-specific wording inside the existing template, not changing the template itself.
- Do not print a raw URL to Andrew's personal Family Archive/AI project in the cover letter unless Andrew explicitly asks for the URL to appear. It may be described as supporting evidence without the direct link.
- Preserve Andrew's primary positioning as a hands-on full-cycle international sales and new-business professional. AI/personal-project evidence is supportive, never the main career identity.
- OUTPUT NAMING: final PDF filenames must NEVER include MASTER or TEMPLATE. Default cover filename: Andrew_Langmaid_Cover_[Company].pdf. Add a short role suffix only when needed to distinguish multiple applications to the same company.
- Before delivery, visually render and inspect the completed one-page cover against the established template.

MANDATORY OPENING EXPLANATION

Every advertised-role cover letter must explain Andrew's reason for applying using this exact sentence unless Andrew explicitly changes it:

"My role with Smartvel is coming to an end, and I am deliberately looking for a permanent, hands-on international sales and new-business position."

Do not replace this with generic wording such as "organisational changes", "post-acquisition restructuring", "seeking a new challenge" or similar. Do not invent a different reason for leaving.

APPROVED CONTENT STYLE - LOCKED

This section supersedes the earlier practice of forcing the same four-part commercial story into every advertised-role cover. The governing standard is now the concise, role-specific, natural style Andrew approved in September 2026.

The cover must read like a senior commercial professional wrote it for this particular vacancy, not like a template, a second CV or AI-generated application prose.

MANDATORY CONTENT SEQUENCE

1. Opening
- If the source is known and natural to mention, say where Andrew saw the role, for example: "I spotted the [role] at [company] on LinkedIn..."
- In the same opening paragraph, state in plain language why the actual work caught his attention.
- Then include the mandatory Smartvel sentence verbatim.
- Do not open with generic praise of the company or phrases such as "I am excited to apply", "thrilled", "perfect fit", "dynamic organisation" or similar filler.

2. Selective evidence only
- Choose the 2-4 strongest proof points for this vacancy from the MASTER CV.
- Do not automatically include entrepreneurship, earned trust, Padani, ArrivalGuides statistics or every stage of Andrew's career. Use only what helps this specific application.
- Bullets are optional. Use them when they make evidence easier to scan, normally no more than four. Every bullet must earn its place.
- A short bold lead-in inside a bullet is acceptable when useful, but do not build the cover from multiple bold mini-heading sections.
- Do not repeat whole CV paragraphs. Translate evidence into the relevance of the role.

3. Bridgeable gap
- If a genuine Tier-B gap exists, acknowledge it directly in one short, natural paragraph.
- State what is new to Andrew without apologising or pretending otherwise, then give the closest truthful transfer evidence.
- Do not over-explain the gap and do not manufacture sector experience, buyer relationships, technical credentials, quota history or language ability.

4. Close
- End with a simple statement of why the role is attractive and that Andrew would welcome a discussion.
- Keep the close human and understated. Do not repeat the advert back to the employer.
- Where genuine autonomy/ownership is central to the role, the approved sentence "I am comfortable being given real responsibility and expected to get on with it." may be used, but it is not boilerplate and must not appear unless it genuinely fits.

ROLE-SPECIFIC TAILORING RULES

- The job advert determines the evidence selection. The cover is not a fixed four-section biography.
- Prioritise evidence of hands-on new business, prospecting, discovery, demonstrations, proposals, negotiation, contracts, close, international market development or relevant partnerships according to the role.
- Use specific commercial results where they materially strengthen the case.
- Mention Andrew's CEO mandate only when needed to explain current positioning or when the vacancy makes seniority context relevant. Do not let the CEO title dominate the cover.
- Mention practical AI/personal-project work only where genuinely relevant to the role; never add it as routine colour.
- If a verified named hiring manager/recruiter is available, address that person. Otherwise use a neutral professional salutation. Never guess a name.
- If an application route does not accept a cover letter, do not invent another field in which to force the cover. Provide the CV and application answers required by the form. Create a cover anyway only if Andrew explicitly asks for one for separate use.

STYLE

- One page.
- Carlito regular/bold typography matching the current advertised CV; not Arial.
- Concise, role-specific, mature and commercially credible. Prefer roughly 250-400 words when that is enough; shorter is better than padding.
- Human, direct and understated rather than polished corporate marketing language.
- No generic company praise, hype, filler, clichés or empty enthusiasm.
- No formulaic "why me / why you / conclusion" construction.
- No second-CV chronology.
- No forced use of the same achievements from one application to the next.
- Preserve Andrew's primary identity as a hands-on full-cycle international sales and new-business professional.
- The final test is whether the letter sounds specific enough that changing only the company and job title would NOT make it reusable for another vacancy. If it would, rewrite it.

FINAL RULE

Future advertised-role covers must follow this updated Source together with the 23 October CV-matched cover exemplar and current ADVERTISED_ROLES_CURRENT_V5_2026-10-28 governance. Never use older cover design rules.
