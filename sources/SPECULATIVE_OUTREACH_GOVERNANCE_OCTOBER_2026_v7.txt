COMPANY QUALIFICATION STANDARD

A speculative target should normally demonstrate several of:
- genuine international or multi-market commercial activity;
- a credible B2B, premium-brand, industrial, sustainability, technology or SaaS proposition Andrew could learn and believe in;
- identifiable need for international growth, enterprise sales, business development, market development or channel/distribution work;
- sufficient maturity to plausibly employ a permanent senior commercial person;
- a sales motion compatible with consultative full-cycle selling.

Avoid or hold companies that are:
- highly specialist scientific/clinical/engineering propositions requiring domain credentials Andrew cannot evidence;
- junior/high-volume SDR environments;
- principally Swedish-only/local sales without credible international scope;
- fashion/clothing;
- travel technology/destination content;
- recruitment/staffing/agency businesses;
- social-media/noise-led propositions inconsistent with Andrew's preferences;
- extremely small/immature businesses where a permanent senior role is implausible;
- companies with a live advertised role that should instead be assessed through the advertised-role workflow.
- legal-technology businesses: exclude by default because Andrew regards this category as unusually exposed to generative-AI substitution, feature commoditisation and reduced defensibility. Only reconsider a legal-tech company if Andrew explicitly asks or there is a clearly evidenced, unusually defensible commercial proposition that is not materially dependent on functionality generative AI can readily reproduce.
- other software propositions where generative AI materially weakens the product moat or can readily substitute the core customer value. AI exposure is a qualification factor, not merely a technology label: distinguish durable workflow/data/infrastructure value from easily replicated content, drafting, generic automation or thin-feature software.

RECIPIENT HIERARCHY AND TITLE-ACCURACY LOCK - REVISED 8 OCTOBER 2026

USER OVERRIDE - NON-NEGOTIABLE

For speculative outreach, Andrew does NOT want the message addressed to someone occupying the sales/commercial position that Andrew himself could plausibly fill.

FINAL RECIPIENT RULE
1. CEO is the default recipient.
2. COO is equally acceptable and may be preferred where operations/organisation/hiring ownership makes the COO the clearer executive sponsor.
3. Founder / Owner / Managing Director may be used ONLY when that person is functionally the top executive and there is no distinct CEO/COO route.
4. Do not use a sales, commercial, revenue, growth, partnerships or business-development executive as the final recipient merely because that person is senior.

DO NOT USE AS FINAL RECIPIENT
- CCO / Chief Commercial Officer;
- CRO / Chief Revenue Officer;
- CSO / Chief Sales Officer;
- VP Sales / VP Commercial / VP Revenue / VP Growth;
- Sales Director / Commercial Director / Global Sales Director / International Sales Director;
- Head of Sales / Head of Commercial / Head of Business Development / Head of Growth;
- Sales Manager / Export Sales Manager / KAM / Account Executive / Business Development Manager;
- any other person whose role is materially the same sales/commercial seat Andrew could be approaching the company to fill.

WHY
The recipient should be the executive above the potential commercial hire, not the incumbent in the target seat. Sending Andrew's speculative approach to a sales leader can create a conflict of interest and may route the message to the very person whose role or future remit Andrew could otherwise be considered for.

TITLE ACCURACY - NON-NEGOTIABLE
- Record the person's title EXACTLY as currently published by a current company-controlled source or current LinkedIn profile.
- Verify current employment and current title before a company counts as send-ready.
- Prefer company leadership/team/contact pages; LinkedIn may corroborate.
- If evidence conflicts, HOLD until resolved. Do not infer or upgrade titles.
- Direct professional email verification is separate from title verification. Both must pass.
- Record source and verification date for every final recipient.

RECENT-BATCH CORRECTION RULE
- Any active draft or scheduled speculative message addressed to a sales/commercial/revenue/growth/BD leader must be reviewed under this V7 rule before it is allowed to proceed.
- Where possible, replace the route with the current CEO/COO or functionally equivalent top executive.
- Historical sent mail remains historical evidence; do not rewrite it.
- Do not send a corrective duplicate to a new CEO/COO without Andrew's explicit current approval and a fresh duplicate/history review.
