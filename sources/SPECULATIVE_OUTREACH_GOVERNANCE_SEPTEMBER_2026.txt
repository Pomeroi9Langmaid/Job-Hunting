COMPANY QUALIFICATION STANDARD

A speculative target should normally demonstrate several of:
- genuine international or multi-market commercial activity;
- a credible B2B, premium-brand, industrial, sustainability, technology or SaaS proposition Andrew could learn and believe in;
- identifiable need for international growth, enterprise sales, business development, market development or channel/distribution work;
- sufficient maturity to plausibly employ a permanent senior commercial person;
- a sales motion compatible with consultative full-cycle selling.

Avoid or hold companies that are:
- highly specialist scientific/clinical/engineering propositions requiring domain credentials Andrew cannot evidence;
- junior/high-volume SDR environments;
- principally Swedish-only/local sales without credible international scope;
- fashion/clothing;
- travel technology/destination content;
- recruitment/staffing/agency businesses;
- social-media/noise-led propositions inconsistent with Andrew's preferences;
- extremely small/immature businesses where a permanent senior role is implausible;
- companies with a live advertised role that should instead be assessed through the advertised-role workflow.
- legal-technology businesses: exclude by default because Andrew regards this category as unusually exposed to generative-AI substitution, feature commoditisation and reduced defensibility. Only reconsider a legal-tech company if Andrew explicitly asks or there is a clearly evidenced, unusually defensible commercial proposition that is not materially dependent on functionality generative AI can readily reproduce.
- other software propositions where generative AI materially weakens the product moat or can readily substitute the core customer value. AI exposure is a qualification factor, not merely a technology label: distinguish durable workflow/data/infrastructure value from easily replicated content, drafting, generic automation or thin-feature software.

APPROVED REBUILD METHOD - CONFIRMED 21 SEPTEMBER 2026

When an attached draft must be recreated rather than edited in place:
1. Use a previously sent approved speculative email as the visual reference.
2. Build the outgoing message as HTML, not plain text.
3. Preserve the real HTML bullet list and bold proof-point labels.
4. Reuse the established signature HTML structure.
5. Embed Andrew's approved headshot inline so Gmail stores it as a CID/inline image.
6. Attach the company-specific CV PDF.
7. Create the replacement draft first.
8. Inspect the saved Gmail draft/MIME to confirm inline image + attachment + DRAFT state.
9. Only then remove the malformed/superseded draft.
10. Re-list Gmail drafts and confirm exactly one active draft remains for that company.

This method was successfully validated on the 21 September 2026 speculative batch and is now the required rebuild method for future speculative drafts.


AI / PRODUCT-DEFENSIBILITY CHECK - ADDED 21 SEPTEMBER 2026

Before promoting a new speculative company, explicitly ask whether generative AI can readily reproduce or materially compress the company's core customer value.
- EXCLUDE by default: legal tech; generic drafting/content-generation tools; thin workflow layers; undifferentiated automation where AI can substitute the core product with low switching cost.
- DO NOT exclude merely because a company uses AI. Physical products, infrastructure, proprietary operational data, embedded enterprise workflows, regulated/mission-critical systems, distribution networks and specialist platforms may remain defensible.
- Where exposure is mixed, mark the company HOLD / BORDERLINE rather than silently qualifying it.
- If a company fails this product-defensibility check, do not spend Andrew's time sourcing a professional contact or creating a Gmail draft.
