Things not to do

Do not over-sell too early

  • This is partly discovery for you.
  • Understand the role before claiming it is perfect.
  • Use examples after you understand the problem they need solved.

Do not sound managerial

  • Do not lead with “I managed teams”.
  • Lead with building business personally.
  • Then show you can coach / structure / lead around that.

Do not hide the timing

  • You are still with Smartvel.
  • Expected end date: end of October.
  • That is a clean, normal transition point.
  • No need to sound apologetic.
Good close if it sounds right: “From what you’ve described, that sounds close to the kind of role I’m looking for — hands-on, commercial, and with scope to help shape growth. What would the next step normally be from here?”
If it sounds management-heavy: do not force it. Clarify whether they genuinely need a player-coach. It is better to discover a structural mismatch now than win the wrong job.
