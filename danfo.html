WHY YOU ARE LEAVING

Clean, truthful answer

“My role finishes at the end of October, and that is agreed. I originally took the CEO role to stabilise ArrivalGuides and prepare it for a sale, and that mandate was completed when Smartvel acquired the company. I stayed on to help with the transition and, at the same time, continued doing a lot of hands-on international new business. Over the last 18 months the economics around the content side of the business have become more difficult, with AI and wider market changes being part of that, and the group has had to look carefully at costs. We have had open discussions about it, and it became clear that this is the right point for me to move on. I have done what I originally came in to do, and I now want to put my energy back into a permanent hands-on international sales role.”

Why this works

It is factual, commercially understandable and does not pretend everything was perfect. It explains the business context, confirms the original mandate was completed, and finishes by moving the conversation toward what you want next.

Do not volunteer

“I was sacked”, “I should have left 18 months ago”, “I stupidly stayed because it was comfortable”, or a long explanation about A-kassa. Those are personal feelings, not the useful business explanation.

IF HE PUSHES

Backup version

“The acquisition changed the shape of the role. I stayed longer than the original mandate because I wanted to help the transition and I believed the business could recover. In hindsight I probably stayed a little too long, but there is nothing dramatic behind it. The company needs to reduce cost, my original mandate is complete, and the timing is right for me to move back into the kind of international new-business role I actually want to do.”

QUESTIONS TO ASK

Find the real commercial need.

Do not interrogate him. Pick the questions that fit the flow and listen for the problem he is trying to solve.

  • 01
    “When you mentioned a possible opening in a few months, what kind of need do you think may emerge?”
    Best opening question. Let him define the role/problem.
  • 02
    “Would that be primarily within Sweden, or are you thinking more broadly across the Group and international markets?”
    Critical to understanding your fit.

    “Where do you see the strongest growth opportunity now: the core Danfo offer, services, adjacent products, or new geographies?”

    “How is new business currently generated across the Group — locally in each subsidiary, through business-area managers, or centrally?”

    “How much of the commercial work is formal public procurement versus proactive business development and relationship building?”

    “Are there markets or customer segments where you feel DANFO has a strong proposition but is not yet getting enough commercial traction?”

    “If you could add one commercial capability to the Group today, what would make the biggest difference?”
