BONUS DRAFT

Reward signed business. Not a complicated scorecard.

One clear calculation

Deal commission = agreed commissionable deal value × negotiated rate × contract-term multiplier.

Agree the value definition before the rate: first-year contracted gross profit for equipment/projects or first-year recurring revenue for a pure service agreement. Choose one basis suitable to the role and DANFO margins.

Proposed multi-year multipliers

1 year: 1.00×
2 years: 1.25×
3 years: 1.50×
5+ years: 2.00×

Illustrative negotiation draft only. Apply to genuinely committed, non-cancellable terms; not optional extensions.

When to pay

Earned: on a binding, qualifying contract.
Proposed payout: 50% next monthly payroll after signing, 50% next payroll after first customer payment.

Agree limited cancellation/non-payment adjustments, continued entitlement to earned commission after departure, and a fair documented closing-deal tail. No routine wait until year-end.

Example only — not an agreed commission rate

At 500,000 kr first-year commissionable deal value and a hypothetical 5% rate: 1 year = 25,000 kr; 3 years = 37,500 kr; 5 years = 50,000 kr. The real rate depends on project economics, responsibilities and normal deal sizes.

Any annual strategic bonus should remain separate from earned deal commission. Keep definitions, deal credit, payment dates and rules short and explicit.
