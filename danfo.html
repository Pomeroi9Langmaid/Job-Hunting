THE SIMPLE VERSION

Not “selling toilets”. Selling public-infrastructure solutions and long-term service.

DANFO designs and manufactures public sanitation solutions, then supports customers through installation, renovation, operation, cleaning, maintenance and full-service contracts. The Group also has mobile, dry-sanitation and specialist narcotics-separation products.

Danfo

Permanent public-toilet buildings, design, construction, renovation and lifecycle service.

Danfo360

Full-service model: Danfo can own the building and handle construction, daily operation and maintenance for a fixed monthly cost.

Hyrtoaletten

Mobile / temporary sanitation for construction, events, beaches and outdoor operations.

Pacto + NaRQ

Pacto: dry sanitation without water/electricity. NaRQ: hygienic narcotics-separation for prisons, detention and airports.
